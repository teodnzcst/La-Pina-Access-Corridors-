About the data

Elevations come from your Copernicus GLO-30 tile (REGVEN / UTM 20N), bilinearly sampled every 25 m along the corridor lines in Ayacucho 3 y 4 Rev 31082026. Climb and descent use a 125 m moving average to drop pixel noise. Max grade is measured over 200 m windows. Valley depth is topographic prominence (12 m minimum).

GLO-30 is a surface model: over morichal and gallery forest it measures the canopy, not the ground. Valley floors at the river crossings may be several metres lower than shown, and their depth may be understated. Check against field vertices before using these numbers for drainage design.

The Google Earth profile for Ruta TM A4 runs east to west (0 km = La Piña). Here it is reversed so both corridors share the same origin and direction.
